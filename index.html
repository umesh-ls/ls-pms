What it fixes

Every appraisal so far has rested on recollection. FY26 showed the cost — 42 of 73 people rated 4 or 5, ratings normalised centrally without managers knowing, and nothing behind the increment conversation.

Nothing accumulatedPerformance was assessed once a year, from memory. Now it is recorded monthly and scored quarterly.
No evidence requirementA written example was needed at 5 but not at 4, so 4 became the free answer. Now it is mandatory at both.
Nothing objectiveEvery input was opinion typed into a form. Project allocation now sits beside the rating as factual context.

The rules, in short

Six competenciesDelivery & Accountability and Technical / Functional Skill carry double weight. Divisor is 8, not 6.
Per-function definitionsTechnical / Functional Skill and Quality of Work mean something different in Engineering, Sales, HR, Finance and IT. One framework, not five.
Self-rating excludedCaptured and shown beside the manager’s rating to surface the gap, but it never enters the score.
Months close with their quarterA missed October line can still be added in December. The cycle administrator closes the quarter and the record is then fixed.
42-day eligibilityLess than that with the rating manager and the quarter is Not applicable — excluded from the annual average rather than counted as weak.
Equal-weighted annualThe simple average of the quarters actually rated, rounded for the existing rating × grade increment matrix.

Before a build starts

Three blockers, all outside engineering’s control until resolved.

  1. Employee ID collision. One code identifies a different person in each of the two systems, and thirteen people carry different IDs across them. The HR system’s ID must win everywhere before any join is written.
  2. Project → rating manager map. Allocations exist but no system records who leads a project. All 18 need a named lead; two have none obvious.
  3. Stale record. One employee is still marked Active who should not be.
Data notice. The prototype embeds names, designations, grades, reporting lines and project allocations for all 109 active employees. It carries no salary, CTC, identity-document or contact data. This repository is private and should stay that way.
